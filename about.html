My story

The hardest part of software isn’t the code.

My career started at Hyland Software, showing businesses how process automation could replace manual work. From there I spent seven years leading product at AT&T/DirecTV — on DirecTV Everywhere, WatchTV and HBO Max apps used by millions of subscribers — and then consulted for Sony PlayStation, PG&E, ITS Logistics and Salesforce.

Along the way I learned that the hardest part is understanding the problem well enough to solve it simply. That’s the part I love: sitting with the people who do the work, mapping how it really happens, and turning it into something clear enough to build.

In 2025 I started Built by Jessica to bring that same approach to small businesses. Today I build with modern AI tools, so ideas become working software faster than ever.
