Who it’s for

For the “wouldn’t it be great if…” ideas.

  • Founders testing a new product idea
  • Business owners who want to see a tool before paying to build it
  • Teams pitching an idea to leadership or investors
  • Anyone who needs to make an idea real enough to get feedback
What you get

Something real to react to.

  • A 90-minute kickoff workshop
  • A one-page product brief: the problem, the users, the goals
  • A clickable prototype of the key screens
  • One round of feedback and revisions
  • A recommendation on what to build first — and what can wait
  • A rough estimate for building it
The two weeks

Ten working days, four steps.

Day 1

Kickoff

We workshop the idea: who it’s for, what problem it solves, what success looks like.

Days 2–4

Brief & flows

I write the product brief and map how people will move through it.

Days 5–9

Build the prototype

The key screens come to life, with a mid-point preview for your input.

Day 10

Review & next steps

We walk through it together, make revisions, and I share my build recommendation.
