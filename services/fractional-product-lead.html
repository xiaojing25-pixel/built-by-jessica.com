Who it’s for

You need product leadership, but not a full-time hire.

  • Startups that need a product leader but aren’t ready for a full-time head of product
  • Teams between product hires that can’t let the roadmap stall
  • Companies whose product owners or managers need hands-on coaching
  • Organizations adopting Agile or SAFe that want someone who has run it
What you get

Clear direction, a healthy backlog, a confident team.

  • Product vision and roadmap tied to your business goals
  • A prioritized, refined backlog
  • User stories with clear acceptance criteria
  • KPIs and success metrics for each initiative
  • Stakeholder alignment and regular progress updates
  • Coaching for your product owners and managers
  • Support for sprint planning, refinement and demos
How it works

From first call to fully embedded.

1

Free consult

A short call about your goals, your team and what isn’t working.

2

First two weeks

I meet the team, review your roadmap, backlog and metrics, and share a 30/60/90-day plan.

3

Embedded, part-time

I join your standing meetings and work in your tools — Slack, Jira, Confluence — between them.

4

Monthly review

We check progress against goals and adjust the focus as you need.
