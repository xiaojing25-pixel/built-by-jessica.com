Who it’s for

You have the idea. You need the details.

  • Anyone who has tested an idea with a prototype and is ready to build it
  • Teams with a feature idea but no product manager to define it
  • Developers who keep coming back with clarifying questions
  • Features with tricky rules, compliance needs or many types of users
  • Business owners working with freelance or offshore developers
What you get

Everything your developers need to start.

  • Discovery sessions with your users and stakeholders
  • User flows for the normal path, alternatives and exceptions
  • Requirements and business rules, written plainly
  • User stories with acceptance criteria, ready for Jira
  • Success metrics so you know whether the feature worked
  • A handoff walkthrough with your developers
  • Follow-up answers to developer questions during the build
